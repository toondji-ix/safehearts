Our story

Education with a whole lot of heart.

We created the school we wanted for our own children: ambitious in learning, generous in spirit, and warm at heart.

How it started

One classroom. One big belief.

In 2009, Safehearts began with a simple conviction: children learn best when they feel safe, happy, and understood.

Today, our family of schools carries that same promise into every classroom. We combine excellent teaching with thoughtful care, creating a learning experience that develops the whole child — academically, socially, and emotionally.
